## 1. What do the State Council "AI+" opinions, MIIT policies and 15th Five-Year Plan documents say about manufacturing AI, industrial data, platforms and talent?

### Takeaway
National policy in 2025-26 explicitly pursues **both** tracks at once: a small number (3-5) of general-purpose large models pushed deep into manufacturing plus "full-coverage" industry-specific models, and in parallel hard numeric programmes for industrial datasets (100), scenarios (500), agents (1,000), benchmark firms (1,000) and platform/device connectivity. The 2026 follow-up actions ("model-data resonance", industry dataset plan) show that officials themselves diagnosed data, not models, as the binding constraint.

### Cited Findings
- **State Council "AI+" Opinions (国发〔2025〕11号, published Aug 2025).** Three stages: by 2027 AI deeply integrated with 6 key fields and penetration of "new-generation intelligent terminals, agents and similar applications" >70%; by 2030 >90% and the "intelligent economy" an important growth pole; by 2035 a full "intelligent economy and society" stage. Six actions (science & technology, industrial development, consumption, people's welfare, governance, global cooperation); manufacturing sits under "industrial development" ("all-factor intelligentisation of industry"). The 70%/90% targets are economy-wide, not manufacturing-specific. Reliability A. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/102794683.html); [10jqka summary](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250827/c670668652.shtml)
- **"AI + Manufacturing" Special Action Implementation Opinions** (MIIT, CAC, NDRC and others, 8 ministries, published ~7 Jan 2026). Targets for 2027: 3-5 general-purpose large models deeply applied in manufacturing; "distinctive, full-coverage" industry large models; 100 high-quality industrial datasets; 500 typical application scenarios; 1,000 industrial agents; 2-3 ecosystem-leading firms; 1,000 benchmark enterprises; "secure and reliable supply" of core AI technology. Seven task groups (innovation base, intelligent upgrading, product breakthroughs, entity cultivation, ecosystem, security, international cooperation). Has an annex "Guide to AI application for manufacturing enterprises". Reliability A. — [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260107/1286279b91ac4758841b6d56e80a49af/c.html); [gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/202601/content_7054199.htm); [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/102997978.html)
- CSET published an English translation and characterises the two annexes as unusually detailed, for a national-level Chinese AI policy, on how large models are to be deployed in specific industries (new materials, aerospace, pharma, biotech, semiconductors, software); the policy also calls for a large-model evaluation benchmark system with regularly published results. — [CSET translation t0667](https://cset.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/t0667_ai_manufacturing_opinions_EN.pdf) (not fetched; via search summary)
- **MIIT "Industrial Internet and AI integration empowerment action plan"** (Jan 2026): by 2028 at least 50,000 enterprises to carry out "AI + industrial internet" upgrades. Reliability A. — [Wallstreetcn](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3763172)
- **MIIT "Action plan for high-quality development of industrial internet platforms (2026-2028)"** (issued 13 Jan 2026): by 2028 >450 platforms "with a certain influence", industrial device connections >120 million units/sets, platform penetration rate >55%. Reliability A. — [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260114/430dc3af7fb7437982accae23f806def/c.html)
- **15th Five-Year Plan outline (2026-2030, adopted March 2026)**: makes "AI+" a headline action; calls for manufacturing "intelligent transformation, digital transformation and network connection" (智改数转网联) and for implementing a Smart Manufacturing Project and an Industrial Internet Innovation Development Project; anchored to "basically achieving new industrialisation by 2035". NDRC estimate: AI-related industry scale to exceed RMB 10 trillion by end of the 15th FYP (reliability A/B, a projection). — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103191284.html); [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20260413/7d240ad3fc8146908e79f45ee2b915bc/c.html) **[URL attribution uncertain]**
- Local targets differ and must not be merged with national ones: Hubei targets 100 industry vertical large models, 100+ industrial agents, 50+ industrial datasets; Shanghai targets 100,000 humanoid robots in factories and >80% agent adoption among above-scale industrial firms by end of 15th FYP. Reliability A (aspirational). — [PEdaily](https://news.pedaily.cn/202604/562844.shtml); [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103191284.html) **[URL attribution uncertain]**
- **2026 "Model-Data Resonance" (模数共振) action** (MIIT General Office + National Data Administration, 工信厅联科函〔2026〕193号, 28 Apr 2026): covers 20 key industries (steel, autos, medical equipment, electronic components, etc.); seven tasks including industry "general-knowledge" and "specialist-knowledge" datasets, model evaluation mechanisms, "model-data resonance spaces", innovation consortia, key cities; goal is a "data - model - scenario" virtuous cycle. Provincial plans due 30 May 2026, mid-term summary 30 Aug 2026, final report 30 Nov 2026. Reliability A. — [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/info/20260428/d6fe90717e7048d5be05823c8cadfdb4/c.html); [NCSTI text](https://www.ncsti.gov.cn/zcfg/zcwj/202605/t20260506_245797.html)
- An English policy commentary describes the same action as MIIT's response to "data infrastructure bottlenecks": data disconnected from models, blocking large-scale industrial AI deployment; abundant national industrial data but little coordination across firms, platforms and regions. — [PolicyCN](https://policycn.com/commentaries/miit-launches-initiative-to-address-data-infrastructure-bottlenecks-56076)
- Unconfirmed per-industry KPIs for that action (≥5 general datasets, 1 industry model, 30 high-value scenarios per industry; ≥3 "resonance spaces" per province) appear only in a product-manager blog. Reliability D, **uncertain**. — [Woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/ai/6424343.html)
- Smart-factory tier programme: AI application capability is now part of the assessment criteria for "Excellence" and "Leading" tier factories, with thresholds to rise over time (per CCTV, relayed by trade press); 2026 tier cultivation notice is 工信厅联通装函〔2026〕343号 with 2026 edition criteria. — [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/06/08/china-builds-43000-smart-factories-as-ai-becomes-a-mandatory-benchmark-for-top-tier-plants/); [NCSTI](https://www.ncsti.gov.cn/kjdt/tzgg/202607/t20260720_251553.html)

### Inferences
- Policy wording does not choose between "reusable foundation model" and "site-specific data/engineering": it sets a quota for general models (3-5) and simultaneously mandates industry models, datasets and scenarios. The sequencing is telling, though: model targets came first (Jan 2026), and within four months a dedicated data-model coupling action followed, implying the centre saw models outrunning usable industrial data. **[mixed, leaning supports H on the data side]**
- The cap of "3-5" general models reads as an attempt to consolidate rather than multiply foundation models, i.e. the state wants reuse of a few bases and differentiation at the industry/data layer.
- All of these are targets. None of the 2027/2028 numbers can yet be assessed for achievement as of Nov 2026; the "resonance" final reports are only due 30 Nov 2026.

### Gaps
- Exact text of the AI+Manufacturing Opinions on small models, distillation, fine-tuning, SMEs and talent could not be read (CSET and Xinhua hosts blocked). Whether the document uses "大小模型协同" language is unconfirmed.
- No quantified data-element (数据要素) target specific to manufacturing was found in the 15th FYP outline.
- Talent clauses of the State Council AI+ Opinions and MIIT documents were not retrieved.

## 2. Who builds industrial large models and how are they deployed (generic model + fine-tune vs per-scenario small models)? Reported results and failures

### Takeaway
The dominant deployed pattern is a hybrid: a vendor foundation model (Huawei Pangu, Haier Kaos Tianzhi, Midea's "factory brain") as base, fine-tuned or wrapped with plant data, running alongside hundreds of small task models and, increasingly, agents. Published successes are almost entirely company self-reports; the best-documented counter-evidence to H is visual inspection, where one large CV model replaced ~150 station-specific small models with only 50-100 images per station.

### Cited Findings
- **Huawei Pangu.** Pangu 5.5 (Huawei Developer Conference 2025) was claimed to be deployed in 30+ industries and 500+ scenarios; a Pangu prediction model fuses tabular, time-series and image data for cross-scenario generalisation. Huawei's industry solution pages describe the method as "general large model as base + industry knowledge and scenario data for training/fine-tuning" (e.g. intelligent coal blending). openPangu 2.0 was released June 2026. Reliability C. — [Huawei Cloud](https://www.huaweicloud.com/cloudplus/twentiethphase/detail11.html); [Huawei smelting solution](https://e.huawei.com/cn/industries/mining/intelligent-smelting-solution); [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260612/c677424179.shtml) **[contradicts H in stated design: reuse + fine-tune]**
- **Nanjing Iron & Steel (NISCO) + Huawei, "Yuanye" steel model** (reported Oct 2026): 300+ AI models deployed across plants, 1,000+ low-code agents/lightweight apps built by front-line employees, 25 business agents live (process optimisation, visual inspection, scheduling, operations); claims >RMB 100 million annual savings. Reporting notes NISCO did not disclose how the savings were calculated. Source is partly sponsored content. Reliability C, savings figure **unverifiable**. — [Fierce Network (sponsored)](https://www.fierce-network.com/sponsored/expertise-intelligence-nisco-deploys-worlds-first-swarm-intelligence-steel-model-scale); [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/nisco-reports-over-cny100-million-annual-savings-from-huawei-774d0644) **[mixed: one industry model, but 300+ models and 1,000+ worker-built apps show heavy on-site work]**
- **JAC (江汽集团) Zunjie/Maextro super factory, Hefei, with Huawei Pangu CV model** (2026 press): previously ran 150+ scattered small inspection models; moved to a unified model where 50-100 sample images per station suffice for fine-tuning; claimed defect interception rate 99.99%; coverage of 1,500+ inspection scenarios. Reliability C. — [10jqka / Shanghai Securities News, 30 Jul 2026](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260730/c678532268.shtml); [Science and Technology Daily, 24 May 2026](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-05/24/content_521631.html) **[URL attribution uncertain]** **[contradicts H: strongest reuse/fine-tune case]**
- A general architecture described in trade press for inspection is "small vision model for fast screening + large model for judgement" (collaborative, not replacement). — [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260730/c678532268.shtml) **[mixed]**
- **Haier Kaos (COSMOPlat) / Tianzhi industrial model.** Ranked "Leader" and first on strategy in IDC's "China industrial large model and agent solutions 2025 vendor assessment"; first among national "dual-cross" platforms for seven consecutive years; at WAIC 2026 launched a full-stack industrial AI solution, an "industrial world model" and agent clusters. Case: Xinjin Group compressed-air station retrofit, 30% energy saving and 26,000 t lifetime carbon reduction. Reliability C (ranking is IDC, a paid-analyst product). — [Haier](https://www.haier.com/press-events/news/20251107_282318.shtml); [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3398414500530564); [Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14809270.html)
- **Midea.** Jingzhou washing-machine plant: 14 agents covering 38 core production scenarios coordinated by the "Midea factory brain", combining large models with embodied robots; first-article inspection cut from 15 minutes to 30 seconds using AI glasses plus a quality agent; average efficiency gain 80%, scheduling response 90% faster. Midea Cloud president Jin Jiang: target ~RMB 900 million cost reduction from AI applications in 2026. Reliability C. — [Science and Technology Daily, 13 Jul 2026](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-07/13/content_546653.html); [Yicai Global](https://www2.yicaiglobal.com/news/chinas-manufacturers-take-ai-from-office-to-factory-floor-for-bigger-savings) **[supports H in the sense of in-house, own-plant build by the manufacturer]**
- Industry framing for 2026: industrial internet entering a stage built on three pillars (industrial agents, industry large models, industrial knowledge graphs); IDC analyst Du Yanze says agents versus copilots is an architectural migration, not substitution. — [36Kr, Jul 2026](https://www.36kr.com/p/3906624598463625)
- **Reported failures / limits.** (a) An auto-parts firm applying a general vision model to highly reflective parts saw the false-detection rate rise to 20%; a chemical firm's process-optimisation model, lacking process-mechanism knowledge, recommended parameters that raised energy use. Reliability D (Toutiao repost, unnamed firms). — [Toutiao](https://www.toutiao.com/article/7597271120001204751/) **[supports H, weak evidence]**. (b) The same article's headline "74% of projects fail" has no traceable origin: **do not cite as fact**.
- Industrial large models are still mostly delivered project-by-project ("one factory, one solution"); replicating to a second factory usually requires redoing data and process adaptation. — [ReadAITime industry page](https://www.readaitime.com/industry/manufacturing) (reliability D) **[supports H]**
- 21st Century Business Herald (30 Oct 2026), headline thesis: "industrial large models move to the production site; data and process determine depth of deployment". Low fault tolerance: a device anomaly involves temperature, vibration, raw-material batch etc.; without evaluation standards for false alarms, misses and downtime loss, fluent model output cannot enter the production flow. Recommends starting from bounded tasks (quality inspection, knowledge retrieval, maintenance) before scheduling and process optimisation. — [21jingji](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20261030/herald/db5ae798dd1fa61817b94c277c9049ea.html) **[supports H]**
- WAIC 2026 round-table named "the gap from pilot to scale" as the core issue, with three hurdles: heterogeneous data, near-zero fault tolerance, and closed-loop/ROI. — [China Daily partner feed](http://ex.chinadaily.com.cn/exchange/partners/82/rss/channel/cn/columns/snl9a7/stories/WS6a632985a310d709c2fbf992.html)
- China has 101 WEF Global Lighthouse Network factories after 16 were added on 15 Jan (year per Yicai Global article; ~70% of that batch), the most of any country. Reliability: WEF list is third-party but selection is application-based. — [Yicai Global](https://www2.yicaiglobal.com/news/china-has-most-lighthouse-factories-globally)

### Inferences
- Vendors (Huawei, Kaos) sell the reuse story: one base model, few-shot fine-tune per station. Manufacturers' own accounts (NISCO 300+ models and 1,000+ worker-built apps; Midea 14 agents over 38 scenarios) show the delivered system is a large population of plant-specific artefacts on top of that base. Both statements are true at different layers.
- Evidence for reuse is strongest in perception tasks (visual inspection), where data is images and transfer is known to work; evidence for site-specific data and process knowledge is strongest in control, process optimisation and prediction, where mechanism models and time-series from specific equipment dominate.
- No case found of a model trained in one industry being reused successfully in another without substantial local data; the reported "cross-scenario" reuse is within a plant or within one industry.
- Almost no independent verification exists for any efficiency figure above. The pattern of round numbers (80%, 90%, 99.99%) is typical PR and should be treated as indicative only.

### Gaps
- No usable 2026 material was found on Baidu, Alibaba, iFlytek, Sany/Rootcloud, CATL, BYD or Xiaomi's dark factory in this pass (searches returned nothing specific). Do not infer their approach from the cases above.
- No named, documented failure case (with company and numbers) was found; failures appear only as anonymised anecdotes.
- Contract structure (how many vendor engineers are stationed on site, project duration, price) was not found, so the "engineering manpower" share of deployments is unquantified.

## 3. How is industrial data secured (platform connections, data-element policy, dataset programmes, labelling bases, SOE mandates)?

### Takeaway
China is building industrial data supply administratively and at scale: ~100 million devices on key industrial internet platforms, 120,000 "high-quality datasets" counted nationally, seven pilot labelling cities with 140,000 workers, and SASAC-directed release of SOE scenarios and datasets. The headline counts are official self-reports with undefined quality thresholds, and no manufacturing-only breakdown was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Device connectivity (status, Jan 2026, MIIT Minister Li Lecheng):** industrial internet applied across all 41 major industrial categories; >8,000 5G factories; devices connected to key industrial internet platforms >100 million units/sets; >340 platforms "with a certain influence"; 2025 industrial internet core industry scale expected >RMB 1.6 trillion, driving ~RMB 2.5 trillion of industrial value added. Reliability B (the "driving" figure is a model estimate). — [C114](https://m.c114.com.cn/w16-1303924.html); [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260114/c673990096.shtml); [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260114/430dc3af7fb7437982accae23f806def/c.html)
- 2028 target is 120 million connected industrial devices: only ~20% above the reported current level, and the two figures may use different scopes ("key platforms" vs "industrial devices"). — [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260114/430dc3af7fb7437982accae23f806def/c.html)
- **Smart factories (cumulative as of ~Feb 2026, since the 2024 start of the tier programme):** 15 Leading-tier, 500+ Excellence-tier, 8,000+ Advanced-tier, 35,000+ Basic-tier (≈43,000 total). Basic tier = digitalisation, Advanced = digital + networked, Excellence = intelligent upgrading, Leading = "future manufacturing modes". Reliability B (tiers below Excellence are locally certified). — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103051287.html); [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/06/08/china-builds-43000-smart-factories-as-ai-becomes-a-mandatory-benchmark-for-top-tier-plants/)
- **High-quality datasets (National Data Administration):** 120,000 datasets nationwide by end of June 2026, total volume >1,565 PB, up >60% from Q1; end of Q1 2026: >116,000 datasets, >960 PB. Covers research, industrial manufacturing, health, education. No industrial-only count. Reliability B, low: "high-quality" is self-declared and a 63% volume jump in one quarter with only 3% more datasets suggests reclassification or a few very large additions. — [IT Home](https://www.ithome.com/0/978/725.htm); [Guangming Online, 18 Jul 2026](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-07/18/content_1304534545.htm) **[URL attribution uncertain]**

- **NDA "Implementation plan for the industry high-quality dataset construction action"** (reported 8-9 Jun 2026): focus on research, industrial manufacturing, low-altitude economy, embodied intelligence; six sub-actions (capacity expansion, labelling, quality, application, management services, value release); target: a batch of application-validated industry datasets by end-2028. Reliability A. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103221104.html); [Xinhua Finance](https://www.cnfin.com/yw-lb/detail/20260609/4423756_1.html)
- NDA head Liu Liehong: high-quality datasets are the basic resource for intelligent upgrading of advanced manufacturing; investment in industry datasets should increase. — [Huanqiu](https://finance.huanqiu.com/article/4RnY9lORYnZ); [NDRC](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fzggw/wld/llh/zyhd/202605/t20260506_1405059_ext.html) **[URL attribution uncertain]**

As of Feb 2026 the Huanxin community held 4,700+ models and 1,200 datasets. Reliability B. — [CLS](https://www.cls.cn/detail/2438260); [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260727/c678466910.shtml); [Xinhua, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260211/e1e5acf574784c1ba125e4101fd2bf98/c.html)
- Chinese trade press on the underlying condition: industrial firms face "data silos, missing standards, uneven quality, security risks and a shortage of hybrid talent"; equipment protocols are not unified and IT/OT integration is hard. — [C114, 22 Apr 2026](https://m.c114.com.cn/w16-1308993.html)
- Jiemian feature headline thesis: for industrial large models "the hammer exists, where are the nails"; data remains the difficulty; >400,000 above-scale industrial enterprises with highly fragmented scenarios. — [Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/article/10578274.html) (not fetched; date unconfirmed, likely 2023-24)

### Inferences
- The state treats industrial data as infrastructure to be produced by programme (platform connection quotas, dataset counts, labelling cities, SOE releases), which is a field-data strategy carried out through administrative mobilisation rather than through per-firm commercial projects. **[supports H on the importance of field data; neutral on model reuse]**
- Dataset counts (120,000) are an input metric and are not evidence of usable factory data: the simultaneous launch of "model-data resonance" and an industry dataset action in 2026 implies officials judge existing datasets poorly matched to models.
- 100 million connected devices measures connection, not data quality or labelled process data; the modest 2028 target (120 million) suggests the emphasis is shifting from connection count to use.
- SOEs are the lever for data release because the state can instruct them; private manufacturers' process data remains proprietary and siloed, which is where the reuse story is weakest.

### Gaps
- No count of industrial (manufacturing) datasets within the 120,000, and no progress figure against the "100 industrial datasets by 2027" target.
- No number of datasets in SASAC's second batch.
- No reliable data on data-exchange transaction volumes for industrial data, or on how many firms actually share process data across company boundaries.
- Data-element (数据要素×) action results for industrial manufacturing were not retrieved.

## 4. Engineer workforce scale and training programmes relevant to industrial AI

### Takeaway
China's raw supply is very large (12.7 million college graduates in 2026, roughly 1.3-1.4 million engineering bachelor's degrees a year) and policy is deliberately pushing graduate engineers into firms (42 national elite-engineer colleges, ~26,000 jointly trained engineering master's/PhD students with mandatory 1-2 years in companies). Chinese sources themselves say high-level and hybrid (AI + process) engineers are scarce.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 cohort of regular higher-education graduates forecast at ~12.7 million, +480,000 on the previous year (MoE forecast Nov 2025; restated by MoHRSS minister Wang Xiaoping at the March 2026 Two Sessions press conference). Reliability B+ (administrative count, generally consistent). — [Guangming Online, 20 Nov 2025](https://m.gmw.cn/2025-11/20/content_1304232603.htm); [Guangming Online, 7 Mar 2026](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-03/07/content_1304367508.htm?source=msn)
- Engineering bachelor's degrees ~1.3-1.4 million per year versus ~130,000-145,000 in the US; with junior-college and all-STEM the figure reaches the ~5 million range. Definitions differ and are not directly comparable. Reliability: secondary/commentary. — [Fortune, 14 Jan 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/01/14/china-graduates-1-3-million-engineers-per-year-us-shortage-ai-tools/); [Huxiu, 11 Feb 2026](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4834206.html)
- Chinese Academy of Engineering consulting study: China's annual engineering graduates exceed one-third of the world total, but high-level, innovative engineering talent is "clearly insufficient". — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/102312385.html)
- Scientists and engineers as a share of manufacturing employment: 3.55% in China (2020) vs 23.2% Germany and 14.2% EU; the manufacturing talent planning guide projected a gap of nearly 30 million in ten key fields by 2025. Reliability: older official/planning figures, quoted second-hand. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/102312385.html) **[URL attribution uncertain]**
- **National Elite (Outstanding) Engineer Colleges (国家卓越工程师学院):** fourth batch of 10 universities announced 19 Dec 2025 (incl. Nanjing, Jilin, Wuhan universities), bringing the total to 42; stated plan of 50. Nearly 26,000 engineering master's and doctoral students jointly recruited and trained by universities and firms, 2,000+ graduated (MoE official Wu Gang, Dec 2025). Engineering master's must spend ≥1 year in a company, engineering PhD ≥2 years. Reliability B. — [Yangtse Evening Post](https://www.yzwb.net/news/jy/202512/t20251219_302271.html); [China Daily](https://govt.chinadaily.com.cn/s/202512/11/WS694a3a9c498e368550339275/training-reform-builds-pool-of-top-engineers.html)
- Tsinghua data cited by SCMP: graduates entering manufacturing and energy rose nearly 20% in one year (top graduates shifting towards factories). — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3345299/manufacturing-talent-boom-why-chinas-smartest-students-are-factory-bound)
- MoHRSS said in Jan 2026 it would issue a document on promoting employment in response to AI's impact (content not found). — [CAC repost of Xinhua](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-01/28/c_1771330784845326.htm)
- 36Kr Research Institute lists a "break in hybrid talent" among four intertwined barriers to industrial large models (with scarce high-quality data, insufficient model reliability, high up-front investment). — [Sina Finance / 36Kr Research, 16 Oct 2026](https://finance.sina.cn/stock/jdts/2026-10-16/detail-inivkncx8203301.d.html?oid=3822565526620616&vt=4)

### Inferences
- Mandatory in-company residence for engineering master's/PhD students is a structural mechanism for putting trained people on the factory floor: a "field manpower" policy instrument that has no real counterpart in the model-side targets. **[supports H]**
- 26,000 jointly trained students is small against 400,000+ above-scale industrial firms; elite-engineer colleges are a quality programme, not a mass solution. The mass channel (vocational "field engineer" programme) could not be quantified here.
- NISCO's 1,000+ worker-built low-code agents suggests firms compensate for scarce AI engineers by turning process workers into builders.

### Gaps
- No figures found for the vocational "field engineer special training plan" (现场工程师专项培养计划) or for AI-specific industrial training headcounts.
- No authoritative current estimate of the industrial-AI talent gap; the "30 million by 2025" number predates the current policy cycle.
- No data on vendor field-deployment engineer headcounts (Huawei "军团", Kaos, Rootcloud).

## 5. Critical assessments: model overcapacity, pilot-itis, low SME adoption, data silos

### Takeaway
Chinese domestic commentary is more specific and more critical than the foreign think-tank material found: the recurring diagnosis is one-factory-one-solution delivery, scarce usable data, near-zero fault tolerance and unclear ROI, with SMEs largely stuck at shallow use. Foreign analysts (MERICS, CSET) stress the scale and strategic threat of the push while noting subsidy waste and provincial duplication.

### Synthesis against the hypothesis (H)
- **Supporting H:** project-based "one factory, one solution" delivery with re-adaptation needed at each new plant; official 2026 actions aimed at data-model mismatch; anecdotal failures of general models on reflective parts and chemical processes; 21jingji's "data and process determine depth"; elite-engineer in-company residence; NISCO's 300+ models and 1,000+ worker-built apps; Midea building in-house.
- **Contradicting H:** national quota of only 3-5 general models to be reused across manufacturing; Huawei's base-model + fine-tune method; JAC/Zunjie replacing 150+ small models with one CV model at 50-100 images per station across 1,500+ scenarios; trend to lightweight, replicable SME packages.
- **Net reading:** China emphasises both, in layers. Reusable base models are being consolidated (few, large, vendor- or state-backed) while the scarce and decisive resources in practice are plant data, process know-how and people on site. Reuse demonstrably works for perception tasks; site-specific data and mechanism knowledge dominate for process control and optimisation. No evidence was found of successful cross-industry model reuse without substantial local data.
